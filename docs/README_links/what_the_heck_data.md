# What the Heck Is This Thing? (Data / ML / Product Teams)

This system is for teams drowning in messy data.

Instead of overwriting records, it accumulates evidence and produces summaries when needed.

## The Core Problem

Traditional systems:
- collapse data too early
- hide uncertainty
- lose history

Even confidence-weighted systems still overwrite.

## The Alternative

- Keep all signals
- Track confidence explicitly
- Separate identity from instances
- Reconcile via policy-driven views

## Why ML Teams Care

- Training data improves over time
- Label confidence matters
- Conflicts are informative

## Why Product Teams Care

- Auditable decisions
- Reversible conclusions
- Fewer silent bugs

## Summary

Stop pretending certainty arrives early.
