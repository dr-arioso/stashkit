# StashKit & Dex Family — Wordmark Notes (for Humans)

This document explains how the StashKit and Dex-family wordmarks work,
and why they look the way they do.

It’s not a marketing guide.
It’s here so future us (and contributors) don’t accidentally “fix” things
that are already correct.

---

## Big Picture

These marks are not decorative logos.
They’re a small notation system.

Each part of a wordmark does a job:
- symbols express structure
- suffixes express engines
- prefixes express role
- color expresses authority

If something looks slightly odd, it’s probably intentional.

---

## The Delta

The inverted triangle symbol (“Converging Delta”) is the primary mark. Its inverted form also represents a funnel or ontological sieve.

It represents:
- convergence
- structure emerging from noise
- definition via constraint

Negative space is part of the geometry.
Nothing is ornamental.

---

## What “Dex” Means

Anything ending in **Dex** is an engine or framework.

Because of that:
- Dex is always darker
- Dex always uses the same letterforms
- Dex always uses the same custom **D**

That D has an internal cutout.
It’s derived from the Delta.
It’s a family marker.

Don’t touch it.

---

## Prefixes (Lexi, Meta, Ground, App…)

Prefixes describe *what kind* of Dex this is.

Rules:
- lighter color
- quieter typography
- no clever tricks

Examples:
- LexiDex → language-facing
- MetaDex → reflective / descriptive
- GroundDex → foundational
- AppDex → applied

If a prefix starts looking more “designed” than Dex, something has gone wrong.

---

## Color (Semantic, Not Vibes)

| Part   | Color | Hex |
|-------|------|-----|
| Prefix | Slate Blue | #5D6F8C |
| Dex    | Deep Navy  | #1F405A |

This is about hierarchy, not aesthetics.

---

## Fonts (and Why They Don’t Ship)

We build wordmarks using:
- **Eras Demi ITC** (most text)
- **Cambria** (μ only)

Then we convert everything to vectors.

No live text.
No font files.
No surprises.

---

## μDex Is Special (On Purpose)

μDex is about identity and transformation.
It comes from “MutaDex.”

μ is:
- a symbol
- an operator
- not a word

So it gets to behave differently.

Rules for μ:
- may use a math-optimized, semi-serif glyph
- may feel slightly off compared to other prefixes
- tightly hands off into Dex

This is intentional.
Identity is not fully normalized.

Nothing else gets this exception.

---

## Optical Tweaks Are Allowed

We adjust:
- kerning
- baseline
- spacing

…by eye, not by grid.

What we don’t do:
- decorative alternates
- repeated flourishes
- per-product redesigns

Consistency beats cleverness.
