# Infraliminal /src — Mark Specification (Draft)

> *Umbrella identity for foundational, infraliminal systems.*

This document codifies the current **visual and semantic specification** for the **Infraliminal /src** wordmark. It is intentionally pragmatic rather than decorative, and is meant to support long‑term use across legal, technical, and executive contexts.

---

## 1. Mark Overview

**Mark text:**
```
Infraliminal /src
```

**Pronunciation (informal):**
> *“Infraliminal source”*

**Role:**
- Umbrella / holding identity
- Namespace and provenance marker
- Not a product logo
- Not a marketing headline

The mark is designed to read as **infrastructural**, not promotional.

---

## 2. Typeface

**Font family:** Eras (Eras Medium)

**Weight:** Medium
- Chosen to convey authority without insistence
- Avoids both demibold assertiveness and light-weight tentativeness

**Style notes:**
- No italics
- No alternate glyph substitutions
- Clean, modern, non-theatrical

---

## 3. Typography & Spacing

### Tracking
- Overall tracking is **considerably closed** relative to default
- Purpose: ensure *Infraliminal* reads as a single construct rather than a term being explained

Further tightening may be applied cautiously, provided legibility is preserved.

### Slash and Baseline Treatment
- The `/` is positioned **slightly above** the baseline
- `src` is positioned **slightly below** the baseline

This treatment:
- Establishes `/src` as a structural delimiter
- Avoids URL or filesystem literalism
- Improves visual balance across the long wordmark

---

## 4. Color Palette

### Primary Text Color — Slate Blue
- **Hex:** `#6B7A99` *(Slate Blue)*
- Applied to:
  - All characters except the central pivot glyph

### Pivot Accent Color — Deep Navy
- **Hex:** `#24324A` *(Deep Navy)*
- Applied to:
  - The central **“l”** in *Infraliminal*

**Rationale:**
- Introduces a subtle visual hinge at the liminal pivot
- Communicates depth without ornament
- Must remain color‑only (no weight or size change)

### Background Color
- **Hex:** `#FFF6D5`

The warm off‑white background:
- Softens the technical palette
- Avoids stark clinical contrast
- Supports executive‑facing and legal contexts

Background substitutions (e.g., white or near‑white neutrals) are acceptable where required, but `#FFF6D5` is the preferred presentation.

---

## 5. Usage Guidelines

### Appropriate Contexts
- Legal entity references
- Documentation headers
- Slide footers or title slides
- Repository / organization identifiers
- MetaDex provenance references

### Inappropriate Contexts
- Loud marketing placements
- Decorative treatments
- Gradient or animated usage
- Weight or color experimentation beyond this spec

---

## 6. Semantic Intent (Non‑Visual)

The mark is intended to communicate:
- Origin rather than output
- Source rather than surface
- Structure rather than spectacle

It should feel:
- Calm
- Enduring
- Technically literate
- Non‑fashionable

If the mark ever feels like it is *trying to persuade*, it is being misused.

---

## 7. Status

This specification is:
- **Provisional but converged**
- Suitable for internal use, documentation, and early legal references
- Expected to remain stable unless the underlying identity strategy changes
