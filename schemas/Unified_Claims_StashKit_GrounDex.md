## I. StashKit — Epistemic Resolution Engine (New Claims)

### Claim 1 (System — StashKit)

1. **A system comprising:**  
one or more processors; and  
one or more non-transitory computer-readable memory devices storing instructions that,
when executed by the one or more processors, cause the system to:

(a) maintain a polyhierarchical ontology defining semantic categories, relationships,
and ontological satisfaction criteria;

(b) iteratively resolve identity of an object by applying one or more skills via
a resolver that interrogates characteristics of the object against the ontology;

(c) dynamically re-enter one or more previously applied skills in response to newly
available signals or confidence thresholds;

(d) determine ontological sufficiency when the object satisfies one or more criteria
defined by the ontology; and

(e) generate, upon ontological sufficiency, a machine-interpretable projection artifact
encoding stabilized meaning of the object.

### Claim 2 (Dependent)

2. The system of claim 1, wherein the resolver applies skills in a non-linear sequence.

### Claim 3 (Dependent — MetaDex)

3. The system of claim 1, wherein the projection artifact comprises a MetaDex including
an epistemic core defining semantic invariants, non-equivalences, and assumption surface areas.

### Claim 4 (Dependent — MuDex)

4. The system of claim 1, further comprising applying a declarative interpretive overlay
relative to the projection artifact, the overlay constraining permissible interpretive
transformations without executing logic.

### Claim 5 (Dependent — GrounDex)

5. The system of claim 1, wherein the ontology and resolver configuration are
domain-specialized to form a GrounDex.
